Twigs

A calm, chronological feed of only the people and publications you choose. No ads, no algorithm.

Twigs is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that brings the accounts you follow on Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, YouTube, Substack and any website with a feed into one timeline, newest first, with nothing added by an algorithm.

How it works

  • Add accounts by searching, by sharing a link to Twigs, or by importing who you already follow.
  • Read posts and articles in one calm timeline that tells you when you're caught up.
  • Group accounts into lists, mute words, and save posts for later.

Importing YouTube subscriptions

If you choose, Twigs can import the channels you subscribe to on YouTube. It asks Google for read-only access, reads your subscription list once so you can pick channels, then gives the access back. See the privacy policy for exactly what's read and how it's handled.

Privacy

Twigs has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics. What you follow stays on your device.
