Help

Answers to common questions about Twigs.

How do I add someone?

In the Accounts tab, tap + to search Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube, Substack, or any website. You can also share a profile, channel, video, or article to Twigs from Safari or another app, and Twigs finds the account or feed.

Why do Instagram and X accounts open in their own apps?

Instagram and X don't let other apps show their posts. Twigs keeps those accounts one tap away instead.

How do I hide posts I don't want to see?

In the Accounts tab, tap the gear, then Muted Words, to hide posts that mention a word or phrase, for a set time or for good. Touch and hold a post to pause that account, or, in a signed-in timeline, to hide one person's posts.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Twigs has no servers or accounts of its own. See the privacy policy.

Something else?

Report a problem or suggest an idea on the issue tracker. A free GitHub account is needed to post there.
